Agri_Empower Structured Curriculum

Our curriculum is professionally structured into progressive learning levels: Foundation → Intermediate → Advanced → Specialisation. Each track is designed to develop practical competence, agribusiness intelligence, and production excellence.

🌱 Crop Production

Vegetables, food crops, cash crops, and medicinal crops. Practical farm management, soil health, and production systems.

🐄 Livestock Production

Poultry, aquaculture, land animals, beekeeping, and snail farming. Focused on productivity, animal welfare, and enterprise development.

🧩 Cross-Cutting Modules

Pest & disease management, post-harvest handling, processing, value addition, and agribusiness markets.
