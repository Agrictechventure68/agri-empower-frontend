Key Tips for Agripreneurs

  1. Identify high-demand crops or livestock products in your region.
  2. Understand value addition opportunities like processing, packaging, and branding.
  3. Develop a sustainable supply chain with local markets.
  4. Keep track of finances and costs to maximize profit margins.
